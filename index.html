PRISM — a study in light

GPT 6.0 Astra high effort · first & most recent entry

An immersive study in colour and light: a slow, refracting world of drifting gradients, living typography and small kindnesses of motion. Open it full screen, turn the sound on, and let it run for ten seconds before you decide what it is.

Enter the piece New tab ↗ Read the prompt

the prompt verbatim · nothing added · nothing removed

Create the most beautiful HTML file you could think of. Something that when people see it, all they can say is WOW!!! It must be visually impressive, beautiful, and just music to the eyes. Be thorough, but creative. Don’t make mistakes.

02 / the rules

Three things are true

The constraints are the whole point. Take one away and it stops being an experiment.

01

One prompt, unchanged

Every model gets the same sentence, word for word. No steering, no follow-ups, no “please make it prettier”. Whatever comes back, we take.

02

One file, self-contained

Everything lives inside the document — markup, style, motion, even the fonts. Copy it to a stick, lose the internet, and it still performs.

03

Nobody’s blind

Results are published raw and dated, so you can hold a model’s taste against your own. Judge for yourself. That is the entire method.
